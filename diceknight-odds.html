English summary

DiceKnight contains paid random items (hero draws). All draws (free, ad, gold, paid card) use the same odds.

  • Class: Knight, Archer, Mage, Rogue, Cleric — 20% each.
  • Stars: ★ 28.57% (bonus 3–4), ★★ 42.86% (bonus 5–7), ★★★ 28.57% (bonus 8–9). Each class × star: 5.71% / 8.57% / 5.71%.
  • Each bonus point goes to HP, Attack, Defense or Luck with 25% each. Heroes with 4–6 stars cannot be drawn.
