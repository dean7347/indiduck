Noticon Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Noticon (노티콘), an Android app published by IndieDuck (인디덕), handles your information. The Korean version is at privacy.html#noticon; if the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Summary

Noticon has no account system and no developer-operated server. Your notifications, icons and settings stay on your device and are never sent to us. The app uses the internet only to show ads from Google AdMob, and it never puts notification content in its ad requests. This app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

3. Why we use it

  • To re-post notifications from the apps you choose with an icon you made
  • To clear a re-posted notification when you reply from it or open the original app
  • To show ads that keep the app free

4. Sharing and transfer

We do not receive your notifications, icons or settings, so we do not share them with or sell them to anyone. When you reply from a re-posted notification, your reply is passed only to the original app, as you asked.

Noticon shows banner ads at the bottom of the Home, app picker and Settings screens, and a full-screen ad after you save an icon, through Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob collects the ad information listed in section 2 directly from your device. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

  • Notification access (required): to receive notifications from the apps you choose and re-post them with your icon
  • Post notifications (required): to show the re-posted notifications
  • Usage access (optional): only for "Clear when app opens"
  • Internet and advertising ID (added automatically by the ads library): only to show ads

You can turn these off at any time in Android Settings.

6. Your choices and deletion

Because all information stays on your device, you control it directly: delete icons in the app, dismiss re-posted notifications, revoke permissions in Android Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything. We hold no copy to delete.

Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Where consent is required by law (e.g. the EEA and the UK), the app shows an ad consent form the first time you reach the Home screen, and you can change your choice at any time under "Ad privacy settings" in the app's Settings. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for personalized ads in the same menu. Personalized ads are not shown in Brazil.

7. Children

Noticon is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.
